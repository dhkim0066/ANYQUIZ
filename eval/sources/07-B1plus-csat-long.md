**Ⅰ. 장문 독해 · 설명문 — 1~3번 / 총 50점**

**[1~3] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.**

A sound designer for a play has a problem that someone making a film can usually avoid. In a film, the camera can show a train coming around a corner. On a small stage, there may be no room for a train, or even for the tracks. The designer has to suggest an event that remains (a) <u>hidden</u>. Yet playing an actual recording of a train is not always the best answer.

Imagine a scene in which a girl waits in a kitchen for her father to return. He is coming by train, but the audience must first notice her reaction to his arrival. A long recording of a railway station may include conversations, birds, and a bell from a nearby shop. These details belong to the real place. On stage, however, they can (b) <u>distract</u> listeners from the one sound that matters. A short whistle may communicate the idea more (c) <u>clearly</u> than the whole recording.

The timing of that whistle matters, too. If it sounds while the girl is busy washing dishes, the audience may notice his arrival before she does. If she puts down a cup and listens just before the whistle sounds, the audience shares her moment of doubt. Moving the sound by only a few seconds can therefore (d) <u>preserve</u> the meaning of the scene, even when the actors say exactly the same words.

The designer also checks the effect from several seats. A whistle that sounds distant near the back wall may be painfully loud in the front row. The aim is not to give everyone an identical experience. It is to make sure the sound serves its purpose from each part of the room. A carefully chosen sound leaves the stage looking exactly the same while giving the audience something new to (e) <u>imagine</u>.

**Ⅱ. 장문 독해 · 이야기 — 4~6번 / 총 50점**

**[4~6] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.**

(A) My grandmother asked me to choose some family photographs for her birthday dinner. We found a box of prints under her bed. In one picture, my mother was learning to ride a bike. In another, my uncle was carrying an enormous cake through the kitchen. The pictures showed every member of the family except my grandmother. She smiled at my surprise. “Well, someone had to hold the camera,” (a) <u>she</u> said. I decided that the empty space on our display should belong to her.

(B) “Your grandmother ran beside me all afternoon,” my mother said over the phone. “When she finally let go, I shouted at her to take a picture before I fell!” My grandmother laughed. “I almost dropped the camera,” (b) <u>she</u> admitted. My mother had another memory about the cake, and (c) <u>she</u> promised to write both stories down before dinner. By the next morning, two handwritten notes had arrived. I placed one below each photograph.

(C) At dinner, guests stopped to read the notes before looking at the pictures again, and I noticed my grandmother reading them too. This time, (d) <u>she</u> agreed to let me take a photograph on one condition. Since she wanted everybody in it, including me, we set the camera on a shelf and hurried into place together. Later, we put that picture in the empty space, with one final note below it. It read, “For once, nobody had to hold the camera.”

(D) My first idea was to take a new picture of her, but my grandmother shook her head. A photograph of herself standing alone would tell the guests little about the pictures in the box. Instead, (e) <u>she</u> suggested asking my mother what had happened just outside each picture. I called my mother and asked whether she remembered the afternoon when the bike photograph was taken. I put the phone on the table so we could both hear her answer.

**정답 및 짧은 해설**

**1 ② · 2 ④ · 3 ⑤ · 4 ① · 5 ③ · 6 ②**

1. **②** — 기적 소리의 선택과 타이밍, 객석별 음량을 통해 무대 음향이 관객의 이야기 이해를 어떻게 이끄는지 설명한다.
2. **④** — 기적 소리의 시점에 따라 관객이 아버지의 도착을 알아차리는 순간이 달라지므로 preserve(유지하다)를 change(바꾸다)로 고쳐야 한다.
3. **⑤** — 같은 기적 소리가 뒷자리에서는 멀게, 앞줄에서는 귀가 아플 만큼 크게 들릴 수 있다고 했다.
4. **①** — (A)에서 할머니 사진이 없는 걸 발견한 뒤 (D)에서 어머니에게 전화를 걸고, (B)에서 들은 사연을 사진 아래 붙여 (C)의 저녁 식사에서 공개한다.
5. **③** — (c)는 사진 속 사연 두 가지를 써 주겠다고 약속한 어머니이며, (a)·(b)·(d)·(e)는 모두 할머니다.
6. **②** — 혼자 선 사진은 옛 사진의 사연을 전하기 어렵다며 할머니가 처음 제안에 고개를 저었다.
